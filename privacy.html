Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026. Kent Brady makes Hushlet. This page explains what data Hushlet collects, how Hushlet uses it, and who receives it.

Data you give us

  • Account: your name and email address. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email that service shares.
  • Your children: name, birth date, sex (optional), and the emoji and settings you choose.
  • Activity you log: sleep, feeds, diapers, baths, tummy time, medicine, growth measurements and milestones.
  • Household: which caregivers are in your household, and the invites you send.

How we use it

We use your data only to run Hushlet: to show your baby's day, sync it to the caregivers in your household, build schedules and insights, and send the notifications you turn on.

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who receives your data

  • Caregivers in your household see the children and activity in that household.
  • Supabase stores your account and household data and runs our servers.
  • OpenAI receives your messages to the AI assistant, along with your child's name and a summary of recent activity, so it can answer. OpenAI processes this data under its API terms, which do not allow it to train models on that data by default.
  • Expo delivers push notifications. A notification can include your child's name and the activity it is about.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in if you choose them.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Location: if you turn on the adaptive night theme, Hushlet uses your approximate location to work out sunrise and sunset. Your location stays on your phone.
  • AI chat history is stored on your phone.
  • Spotlight: Hushlet can add your logged events to Spotlight search on your phone.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. To delete your account and data, email hushlet.baby@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with.

Children

Parents and caregivers use Hushlet. Children do not use it. The data about a child is the data a parent or caregiver enters.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions? Email hushlet.baby@gmail.com.
