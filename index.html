Hushlet Support

Need help with Hushlet? Email hushlet.baby@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

How do I add another child?

Tap the gear on the dashboard, then Manage Household. Tap + Add Child.

How do I invite a caregiver?

Tap the gear, then Manage Household. Tap Invite Caretaker and share the invite. Each invite code works one time.

A caregiver's entries don't show up

Check that both phones are online and in the same household. Entries sync as soon as each phone has a connection.

How do I add widgets?

Touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then add a Hushlet widget.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email hushlet.baby@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with. We will delete your account and data.
